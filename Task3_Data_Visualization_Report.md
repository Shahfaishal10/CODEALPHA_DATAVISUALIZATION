# CodeAlpha Internship — Task 3: Data Visualization

## Objective
Transform the Geldium delinquency dataset into visual formats that reveal insights clearly and support decision-making.

## Dataset
- 500 customers
- 80 delinquent
- 420 non-delinquent
- Delinquency rate: 16%

## Eight Visualizations
1. Delinquency Distribution — shows the 84%/16% class split.
2. Credit Utilization Distribution — shows utilization spread and 90%/100% reference levels.
3. DTI by Delinquency — compares debt burden between groups.
4. Employment — compares observed delinquency rates by employment.
5. Credit Card Type — compares observed rates by card type.
6. Location — compares observed rates by location.
7. Income vs Loan Balance — explores the relationship between income and outstanding debt.
8. Credit Score vs Utilization — explores the relationship between credit score and utilization.

## Conclusion
The dashboard converts raw customer data into an easy-to-understand data story. Credit utilization and DTI are useful financial indicators to monitor. Employment, card type and location show different observed delinquency rates. These patterns are associations, not proof of causation. Counterintuitive payment-history and credit-score findings from EDA should be validated before operational use.
